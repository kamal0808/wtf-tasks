# WTF tasks

WTF's public project repo: tasks and the Neki ledger for [WTF](https://wtf.topc.social), TOPC's anonymous public rant wall where every rant tags an organisation.

- [Issues](https://github.com/kamal0808/wtf-tasks/issues): open tasks. Each issue is one task, and its `neki:N` label is the Neki it earns (1 Neki = 1 rupee of work at market price).
- [`LEDGER.md`](LEDGER.md): who has earned what. Your share of WTF = your Neki / all Neki there.

Code: [kamal0808/wtf](https://github.com/kamal0808/wtf), live at [wtf.topc.social](https://wtf.topc.social). Private for now: you get access when you join the project.

## How it works

1. Pick any open issue. Anyone can hand in, and the best hand-in wins.
2. Do the work however you like, including with your own AI.
3. Hand in by commenting on the issue with a link others can open and a line or two on what you did.
4. Kamal accepts the best hand-in, labels the issue `accepted`, and closes it. The Neki is added as a row in [`LEDGER.md`](LEDGER.md).

## Rules

- Proof must be something others can open, and anyone you quote or record has to agree to it.
- Made-up people, answers or numbers earn nothing.
- Neki is a record of your work and becomes your share in what gets built. Rules: TOPC's NEKI.md.
